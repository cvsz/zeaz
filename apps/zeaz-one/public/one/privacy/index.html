Privacy Policy

Pre-launch privacy framework for the ZEAZ One website, support experience and future product services.

Status: This is a product-development baseline dated August 2026. It must be reviewed by qualified counsel and updated to match the final services, vendors, launch markets and data flows before publication as an operative policy.

1. Our privacy approach

ZEAZ One is designed around understandable controls, data minimisation, user choice and an on-device-first AI architecture. Business content must not be silently uploaded for AI processing.

2. Information this website may process

The production website may process information you submit, such as contact details, support requests, business-enquiry details and product-interest preferences. Standard technical logs may include IP address, browser type, device type, timestamps and security events.

3. How information may be used

  • Operate and secure the website.
  • Respond to product, business and support enquiries.
  • Manage beta, pilot or launch communications when requested.
  • Detect abuse, fraud and security incidents.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

4. AI processing principles

Planned ZEAZ One AI features use three processing tiers: on-device processing, an organisation-approved private cloud and user-approved public-cloud services. Processing location, provider identity and retention choices should be visible where applicable.

5. Sharing and service providers

Information may be shared with contracted infrastructure, communications, support, analytics or security providers only as necessary to provide the relevant service and subject to appropriate contractual safeguards. Final provider names and international-transfer mechanisms must be listed before launch.

6. Retention

Information should be retained only for the period necessary for the stated purpose, legal compliance, dispute resolution and security. Final retention schedules must be documented before launch.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on location, users may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or export personal information and to object to certain processing. A verified request channel must be published before commercial launch.

8. Security

Planned controls include encryption in transit, access control, audit logging, vulnerability management and secure operational processes. No system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

ZEAZ One services are not intended to collect personal information from children without an appropriate legal basis and required consent.

10. Contact

Privacy enquiries should be routed through support.zeaz.dev/zeaz-one. A formal privacy contact and legal entity address must be added before launch.

11. Changes

The operative policy should display an effective date, version history and material-change notice process.
