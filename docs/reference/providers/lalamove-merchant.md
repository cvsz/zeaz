# Lalamove merchant registration — Thailand

Lalamove's public Thailand pages describe consumer and driver services rather
than a complete merchant-document checklist. Do not fabricate one. Use the
merchant type model and collect business identity, tax, bank, address, menu
and any food/alcohol licence required by the business, then confirm the
current Lalamove business onboarding flow before activation.

The requirement renderer must show only rows returned by the provider data
store; unknown rows remain review-needed rather than silently becoming
required.

Reference: https://www.lalamove.com/th-th/
