# Lalamove driver registration — Thailand

## Flow

Download Lalamove Driver, register with phone verification, enter personal and
vehicle data, upload documents, complete any required four-wheel training,
then wait for review and activation.

## Required information and uploads

The Thailand driver page lists Thai nationality and age 18–60, name, phone,
email, national-ID number, vehicle type, make/model, plate and registration
year. It lists national ID, driving licence, annual tax label, first page of a
KBank passbook in the driver's name, vehicle-plate photo and front-facing
profile photo. Four-wheel drivers may submit additional documents after
registration.

## Verification, renewal and additional checks

Lalamove reviews the uploads and may require training for four-wheel vehicles.
The criminal-record page says activated drivers must submit the requested
criminal-history evidence within 30 days or access can be restricted. Renew
licence, tax, bank and criminal-record evidence when requested; do not infer a
renewal period that the provider has not published.

## Supported vehicles and Thailand notes

The public Thailand page covers motorcycle and four-wheel registration and
states that vehicle age must not exceed 10 years. Exact categories and city
availability remain provider-controlled.
