# Grab rider registration — Thailand

## Flow

Use the GrabDriver application or the current Thailand driver-partner signup
page, submit identity/vehicle/banking information, complete any requested
training or screening, then wait for review. The exact product (GrabFood,
GrabExpress or passenger transport) changes the checklist.

## Information and documents

The published Grab document preview identifies the national ID, valid driving
licence, vehicle/registration evidence, profile image and banking information
as documents used for driver onboarding. The application must collect legal
name, phone, date of birth, address, vehicle type and plate separately from
the uploads. Public pages do not promise one universal checklist, so the
operator must confirm the product-specific checklist in the current app.

## Optional, renewal and verification

Public transport products may request public driving-licence or additional
vehicle evidence. Renewal is driven by expiry of the licence, vehicle tax or
insurance; replace the affected document before it expires. Grab reviews the
submitted files and may request correction or additional screening; do not
mark a rider active until the provider result is recorded.

## Supported vehicles and Thailand notes

Food delivery commonly supports motorcycle delivery; car and other products
are product- and city-dependent. Thailand identity and DLT documents must be
current and readable. This repository stores requirements as versioned data,
not as a hardcoded Grab rule.
