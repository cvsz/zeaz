# Grab merchant registration — Thailand

## Flow and business types

Register in GrabMerchant or through the website, submit the store form and
documents, receive the e-contract, sign it, then create the menu and open the
store. The published flow supports GrabFood, GrabMart and GrabKitchen; model
the applicant as individual, company, restaurant, cloud kitchen, cafe, retail
or grocery and select the applicable service.

## Documents

For a Thai individual, Grab lists a current national ID and an active savings
or current account in the owner's name (the published page lists supported
banks). For a Thai company, it lists a company certificate issued within six
months, VAT certificate (ภพ.20), shareholder list (บอจ.5) within six months,
director ID, company bank account and power of attorney where applicable.

Store address, branch, phone, menu and store details are structured fields;
menu and photos are completed during store setup. Food or alcohol licences
must be requested only when the selected goods require them.

## Verification and renewal

Grab states that applications are reviewed and results are sent in the app or
email, commonly within 3–5 business days; rejected files can be corrected and
resubmitted. Company certificates, VAT records, bank evidence and licences
must be renewed when their source authority or provider requires it.
