# Bolt rider/driver registration — Thailand

## Flow

Register through Bolt's Thailand driver-partner flow, select the vehicle and
service, submit the required files, and wait for document review and any
app-based onboarding steps.

## Required information and uploads

Bolt's Thailand support checklist names a driving licence, national ID,
vehicle registration, vehicle photo and driver profile picture. Collect the
driver's legal name, contact details, vehicle make/model/plate and ownership
relationship as structured fields. Ror Yor 17 and Ror Yor 18 are described as
recommended for DLT compliance, not as a universal required upload.

## Conditional/optional documents

If the vehicle belongs to a close relative, Bolt describes a power of attorney
and proof of relationship. A vehicle can be financed, but the registration
book may need to be retrieved for registration. Store these as conditional
requirements keyed by vehicle ownership, never as unconditional buttons.

## Verification, renewal and vehicle notes

Bolt reviews the files before enabling the driver. Keep licence, registration,
tax and any DLT-related documents current; expired documents require a new
upload and review. The published Thailand page describes app-based taxi cars
as one-vehicle-per-driver, owner-name matching/relationship evidence, a
maximum vehicle age and four-door characteristics; courier products may differ.
