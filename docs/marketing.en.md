# Marketing plan template

## Positioning

For `{{TARGET_CUSTOMER}}` in `{{LOCATION}}`, `{{BUSINESS_NAME}}` offers a
clear, credible benefit that competitors do not deliver as simply or reliably.

## Launch loop

1. Publish a simple profile: offer, location, hours, price cue and contact.
2. Invite a small local audience with a specific opening offer.
3. Ask every early customer for a review or referral.
4. Track reach, enquiries, conversion, repeat rate and acquisition cost weekly.

## Content pillars

- Proof: customer outcomes, process and reviews.
- Utility: tips that help the target customer.
- Personality: people, local context and values.
