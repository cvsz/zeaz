# MooPiew marketing plan

## Positioning

MooPiew offers warm charcoal-grilled pork and a simple, reliable way to order
for pickup or a configured local delivery area. Communicate only the active
menu, actual opening hours, approved service area and published price.

## Launch loop

1. Publish the order link, location/service area, hours, menu and a real food
   image with accessible Thai copy.
2. Use a small, time-bounded offer with an owner-approved coupon rule.
3. Ask early customers for feedback after completed orders, not before.
4. Review reach, order conversion, repeat rate, average basket, cancellation
   rate and delivery reliability weekly.

## Content pillars

- Product proof: real grilling, ingredients and current menu items.
- Convenience: pickup timing, clear delivery boundaries and order tracking.
- Trust: hygiene, responsive service and verified customer feedback.

Never publish a customer address, phone number, rider live location, payment
reference or unapproved partner claim.
